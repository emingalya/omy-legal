# OMY Community Guidelines

_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

OMY is for adults (18+) who want real, respectful conversations. Calls only start when both people accept. Breaking these rules can lead to removal of content, hidden profiles, or a permanent ban.

## Be yourself — real identity only

Use your own name, your own photos and your real age. Don’t pretend to be someone else, use another person’s photos or personal details, create fake or duplicate accounts, or misrepresent who you are. Your main photo must clearly show your face, and we may ask you to confirm it’s really you with a quick camera check. Impersonation and catfishing lead to permanent removal.

## Adults only (18+)

You must be at least 18 to create an account or use OMY. Never invite, encourage or pretend to be a minor. If you think someone here is under 18, report them right away. We remove underage accounts and report suspected child exploitation to the appropriate authorities.

## Zero tolerance for harassment and abuse

Treat everyone with respect. Bullying, threats, intimidation, stalking, unwanted repeated contact, sharing someone’s personal information to harass them, and encouraging self-harm are never allowed — on camera, in messages or anywhere else. If someone asks you to stop, stop. You can end a call, report or block anyone at any time with one tap.

## No sexual or explicit content

OMY is not a place for sexual content. Nudity, sexual acts, sexually suggestive behavior, exposing yourself on camera and requests for sexual content are strictly prohibited. Never record, screenshot or share anyone’s image without consent, and never share intimate images of anyone, including yourself. Breaking this rule means an immediate, permanent ban.

## No hate or discrimination

No hate speech, slurs, symbols or content that attacks or demeans people for their race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age or serious illness. No support for violent extremism or terrorist organizations.

## Respect other people’s privacy

Don’t share anyone’s personal information — phone number, address, workplace, financial details or private photos — without their clear permission. Don’t record, screenshot or livestream calls without the other person’s consent. Keep what others share with you private.

## No scams or commercial activity

Never ask for money, gifts, gift cards, crypto, banking or login details. No selling, advertising, promotions, recruiting, sponsored content or links to other services. No romance scams, investment schemes or phishing. If anyone asks you for money, report them — OMY will never ask you for that.

## No spam or platform abuse

Don’t use bots, scripts, automation or fake engagement. Don’t send repetitive or unsolicited messages or try to get around blocks, bans or safety features. Don’t interfere with, reverse-engineer or exploit the app or other people’s accounts.

## Follow the law

Use OMY only for lawful purposes. No illegal goods or services (including drugs and weapons), no violence or dangerous acts, and no content that infringes anyone’s copyright, trademark or other rights. We cooperate with law enforcement when the law requires it.

## How we keep OMY safe

Photos and live video may be checked automatically and by our team to filter objectionable content. You can report a profile or a call from the profile or during the call; we review reports within 24 hours. Depending on how serious a violation is, we may remove content, warn you, limit features, or suspend or permanently ban your account — without notice for serious violations. People you block can’t see or call you. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.
