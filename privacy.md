# OMY Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

This policy explains what OMY ("we") collects, why, who processes it and the choices you have. OMY is operated by **Muhammet Emin Yavuz** (the data controller), Türkiye. Contact: **omy.support@gmail.com**.

## 1. What we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Sign-in identifier from Apple or Google (account id, e-mail if you share it) | Create and secure your account |
| Profile: username, date of birth (age shown, not the date), gender, interests, bio, photos | Show your profile to other adults and suggest people with shared interests |
| Availability (online / in a call) | Let others see when you can take a call |
| Call requests, friend requests, message requests, messages, gifts | Run the features you use |
| Call records: who, when, how long (not the content) | Daily free call time, PRO limits, safety reviews |
| Purchases: App Store transaction ids, coin balance and history, PRO status | Deliver what you bought, handle refunds |
| Device push token | Send notifications you allowed |
| Reports and blocks | Keep people safe and enforce our Community Guidelines |

**We do not record calls.** Live video and audio pass through our real-time video provider only to connect the two people and are not stored. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## 2. Automatic checks

Photos you upload are checked automatically (face detection and nudity / violence detection) before anyone can see them. A photo that fails is deleted and never shown. Reported profiles may be hidden automatically and reviewed by a person.

## 3. Who processes your data

- **Google Firebase** (authentication, database, server functions) — data stored in the EU (Belgium / Netherlands region).
- **Amazon Web Services** (photo storage, delivery and automatic photo checks) — Frankfurt, Germany.
- **Agora** (real-time video and audio routing; nothing is stored).
- **Apple** (Sign in with Apple, payments, push notifications) and **Google** (Google Sign-In).
- **Resend** (sends report alerts to our support inbox).

These providers act on our instructions and may process data outside your country under their standard contractual safeguards.

## 4. How long we keep data

- Your account data is kept while your account exists.
- **When you delete your account** (Settings → Delete account), we delete your profile, photos, username, coin balance, PRO status, call time, devices, friends, open requests and your sign-in right away. Chats you were in are closed and show "Deleted user".
- We keep **reports** made by or about you for up to **2 years** to prevent abuse and repeat bans, and **purchase transaction ids** (without your profile) as required for accounting and refunds.

## 5. Your rights

Under GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to processing, or complain to your data protection authority. Most of this you can do in the app (edit profile, delete account). For anything else, write to omy.support@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

## 6. Age

OMY is only for people **18 and older**. We remove accounts that appear to belong to minors.

## 7. Permissions

Camera and microphone are used only during calls you start or accept. Notifications are optional. You can change these in iOS Settings at any time.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way we will tell you in the app before it applies.
